YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Pressure Washing FAQs

What surfaces can you pressure wash?

We can clean many common exterior surfaces, including concrete driveways, walkways, lanais, patios, walls, and other compatible hard surfaces. We evaluate the surface before cleaning to determine the appropriate method.

Can pressure washing remove algae and mildew?

Pressure washing can be effective at removing many types of dirt, grime, algae, mildew, and organic buildup from suitable exterior surfaces. Results can vary depending on the surface material and severity of staining.

Do you clean driveways and concrete?

Yes. Driveway and concrete cleaning is one of the most common pressure-washing services we provide. We can also clean nearby sidewalks, entryways, and other exterior hard surfaces.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not necessarily. As long as our team has the access and information needed to complete the job, you may not need to remain at the property. We’ll confirm any access requirements when your service is scheduled.

How often should I schedule pressure washing?

There is no single schedule that works for every property. Homes in shaded, damp, coastal, or heavily landscaped areas may experience buildup faster than others. We recommend cleaning based on the condition of your exterior surfaces rather than following an arbitrary timetable.

Do you serve my area on O‘ahu?

Yes. Pau Hana Cleaners serves communities throughout O‘ahu, including Honolulu, Kailua, Kapolei, Wai‘anae, the North Shore, and surrounding service areas.
