What’s Included in Your Move-In / Move-Out Clean?

All Living Areas & Bedrooms

  • Dust ceiling fans, light fixtures, ledges, trim, and reachable surfaces
  • Wipe down baseboards, doors, door frames, and window sills
  • Clean inside closets and shelving in empty rooms
  • Remove dust and buildup from corners and commonly missed areas
  • Vacuum carpets and thoroughly clean hard-surface floors

Kitchen Detailing

  • Clean and degrease countertops, backsplash, and cabinet fronts
  • Wipe inside empty cabinets and drawers
  • Clean sink, faucet, fixtures, and surrounding surfaces
  • Clean microwave inside and out
  • Clean exterior of refrigerator, oven, dishwasher, and other appliances
  • Interior refrigerator and oven cleaning available when included in your estimate

Bathroom Detailing

  • Scrub and disinfect showers, tubs, toilets, sinks, and vanities
  • Clean shower glass, mirrors, fixtures, and chrome
  • Remove soap residue and surface buildup
  • Wipe cabinets, drawers, baseboards, doors, and trim
  • Clean and disinfect floors and high-touch surfaces

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning FAQs

What is included in move-out cleaning?

Our move-out cleaning focuses on detailed cleaning throughout an empty home, including kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, bedrooms, floors, baseboards, fixtures, and other commonly overlooked surfaces. Your exact scope is confirmed with your estimate.

Is interior appliance cleaning included?

Interior appliance cleaning can be included depending on the service scope. Let us know what you need when requesting your estimate so we can quote the job accurately.

Should the property be empty before cleaning?

For the most thorough move-out clean, furniture, boxes, food, and personal belongings should generally be removed before our team arrives.

Do you offer move-in cleaning?

Yes. We clean homes, apartments, condos, and rentals before move-in so you can start with freshly cleaned kitchens, bathrooms, floors, closets, and living spaces.

Which areas do you serve?

Pau Hana Cleaners serves clients across O‘ahu, including Honolulu, Kailua, Kapolei, Wai‘anae, the North Shore, and surrounding communities listed in our service area.
