COMMERCIAL CLEANING SERVICES

A Cleaning Plan Built Around Your Business

Professional cleaning delivered with the care and hospitality of Hawai‘i.

General Cleaning

  • Dust and wipe accessible surfaces
  • Clean desks and common-area surfaces where accessible
  • Wipe high-touch areas, doors and handles
  • Empty trash and replace customer-provided liners
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Spot clean accessible glass and mirrors
  • General tidying of common areas

Entrances & Common Areas

  • Clean entry doors and accessible interior glass
  • Wipe high-touch surfaces
  • Clean reception and waiting areas
  • Vacuum or mop flooring
  • Remove visible dirt and debris

Restrooms

  • Clean and disinfect toilets and urinals
  • Clean sinks, faucets and countertops
  • Clean mirrors
  • Wipe dispensers and accessible fixtures
  • Restock customer-provided supplies
  • Empty trash
  • Sweep and mop floors

Break Rooms & Kitchen Areas

  • Clean countertops
  • Clean sinks and faucets
  • Wipe appliance exteriors
  • Clean tables and eating areas
  • Wipe cabinet exteriors
  • Empty trash
  • Sweep/vacuum and mop floors

Recurring & Additional Services

  • Weekly or multiple-times-per-week service
  • Custom cleaning schedules
  • Deep cleaning
  • Floor deep cleaning
  • Interior/exterior windows
  • Carpet extraction
  • Upholstery cleaning
  • Pressure washing
  • Soft washing
  • Move-in/move-out and turnover cleaning

Commercial scopes vary by property type, square footage, traffic level, and frequency. Pau Hana Cleaners will confirm the cleaning plan and any special requirements before service begins.
