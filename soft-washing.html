THE RIGHT CLEANING METHOD

Gentle Pressure. Thoughtful Care.

Pressure alone is not always the best way to clean an exterior surface. Soft washing uses lower water pressure together with appropriate cleaning solutions to break down organic buildup and surface contamination.

This makes it especially useful for materials that may be more vulnerable to high-pressure cleaning.

Care for Delicate Surfaces

A lower-pressure approach helps reduce the risk of damage to compatible siding, stucco, painted surfaces, and other delicate exterior materials.

Address Organic Buildup

Soft washing is designed to address algae, mildew, mold-like growth, dirt, and other organic staining commonly found on exterior surfaces.

A More Complete Clean

By treating the source of organic buildup rather than only blasting the surface, soft washing can provide a more complete exterior clean.

Made for Island Conditions

Warm temperatures, humidity, rain, shade, and coastal exposure can all contribute to exterior buildup on island properties.

WHICH SERVICE DO YOU NEED?

Soft Washing vs. Pressure Washing

Soft Washing

Best suited for more delicate exterior surfaces and areas affected by organic growth. It relies on low pressure and cleaning solutions rather than high water force.

Common uses may include:

  • Siding
  • Stucco
  • Painted walls
  • Delicate exterior finishes

Pressure Washing

Best suited for durable hard surfaces that can tolerate stronger water pressure.

Common uses may include:

  • Concrete driveways
  • Walkways
  • Patios
  • Some masonry
  • Other durable exterior surfaces

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Soft Washing FAQs

What is soft washing?

Soft washing is an exterior cleaning method that uses lower water pressure and cleaning solutions to remove dirt, algae, mildew, and organic buildup from compatible surfaces.

How is soft washing different from pressure washing?

Pressure washing relies more heavily on water force and is commonly used on durable surfaces such as concrete. Soft washing uses much lower pressure and is better suited to surfaces that may be damaged by aggressive cleaning.

What surfaces can be soft washed?

Depending on the material and condition, soft washing may be suitable for siding, stucco, painted exterior walls, fences, and other delicate exterior surfaces. We evaluate the surface before cleaning.

Does soft washing remove algae and mildew?

Yes. Soft washing is commonly used to treat exterior surfaces affected by algae, mildew, and other organic buildup. Results depend on the material and severity of staining.

Is soft washing suitable for delicate surfaces?

When performed with the appropriate technique and cleaning solution, soft washing is designed to be a lower-pressure option for compatible surfaces. We assess the area before deciding on the cleaning method.

Do you serve my area on O‘ahu?

Yes. Pau Hana Cleaners serves properties throughout O‘ahu, including Honolulu, Kailua, Kapolei, Wai‘anae, the North Shore, and surrounding service areas.
